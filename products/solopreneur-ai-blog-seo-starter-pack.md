## 1. The 10-Minute Keyword Finder
Paste this into any AI chat to get winnable topics:

> "I run a solo business in [NICHE]. List 10 blog keywords with search intent my competitors under-serve. For each: estimated difficulty (1-10), buyer intent (low/medium/high), and one ready-to-rank title under 60 characters."

Pick only keywords scored difficulty 4 or below and intent medium or high. Skip the rest.

## 2. The On-Page SEO Checklist (do all 12)
| # | Must-do | Pass when |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 1 | One primary keyword in H1 | Exact match, no stuffing |
| 2 | Keyword in first 100 words | Natural, once |
| 3 | 3-5 H2 subheads | Each covers a subtopic |
| 4 | 1 internal link | To a related post |
| 5 | 1 outbound authority link | Reputable source |
| 6 | Meta title 60 chars or less | Includes keyword |
| 7 | Meta description 155 chars or less | Includes keyword + CTA |
| 8 | URL slug = keyword | Hyphenated, no stopwords |
| 9 | 1 image + alt text | Alt contains keyword |
| 10 | 300+ words minimum | Aim 800-1,200 |
| 11 | Readable short paragraphs | 3 sentences or fewer each |
| 12 | Clear next-step CTA | Link or opt-in |

## 3. The Blog Post Skeleton (fill the blanks)
```
H1: [Primary keyword] - [Benefit in 6 words]
Intro (100w): [Problem] + [Why now] + [What you will get]
H2: What is [keyword]?
H2: Why [audience] should care
H2: Step-by-step how to [action]
  - Step 1 ...
  - Step 2 ...
H2: Common mistakes to avoid
H2: Quick-start checklist
CTA: [Download / Book / Subscribe]
```

## 4. Ten Copy-Paste AI Prompts
| Goal | Prompt |
|------|--------|
| Title batch | "Give me 8 click-worthy blog titles under 60 chars for the keyword [KW], solo-business tone, no clickbait." |
| Intro hook | "Write a 100-word intro for [KW] that opens with a relatable pain point and ends with the post's promise." |
| Outline | "Create an SEO outline for [KW]: 5 H2s, each with 2 H3s, keyword placed naturally." |
| Draft section | "Write the '[H2]' section for [KW] in plain English, 250 words, one example." |
| Meta title | "Write 5 meta titles 60 chars or less for [KW], include keyword, front-load benefit." |
| Meta description | "Write 3 meta descriptions 155 chars or less for [KW], include keyword + a CTA verb." |
| FAQ | "List 5 FAQ questions for [KW] a beginner asks, with 2-sentence answers." |
| Internal links | "Suggest 3 internal link anchors for [KW] linking to related topics [A,B,C]." |
| Rewrite for readability | "Simplify this draft to 8th-grade reading level, keep keywords, shorten sentences." |
| Repurpose | "Turn this post into 5 tweet threads + 1 Pinterest pin title for [KW]." |

---

## 5. The Meta + Title Formula
- Title: [Primary keyword] + [specific outcome] -> `Email Templates That Book Calls in 7 Days`
- Description: [Outcome] + [proof or scale] + [CTA] -> `Steal 15 copy-paste email templates solo founders use to book discovery calls. Free swipe file inside.`

Front-load the keyword; never lead with your brand.

## 6. Your 7-Day Publish Sprint
| Day | Task | Time |
|-----|------|------|
| 1 | Run Keyword Finder, pick 1 | 10 min |
| 2 | Generate outline + titles | 15 min |
| 3 | Draft 2 sections | 30 min |
| 4 | Draft remaining + FAQ | 30 min |
| 5 | Meta + internal links + image | 20 min |
| 6 | Run On-Page Checklist, fix gaps | 15 min |
| 7 | Publish + repurpose to 5 socials | 25 min |

Repeat weekly. Consistency beats perfection.
