## 3. Ten copy-paste AI prompts

1. "Write 20 subject lines for [offer] using formulas 1-6. Under 55 characters each."
2. "Rewrite this subject line for a skeptical cold list: [paste]. Give 10 variants."
3. "Generate 15 subject lines promising [result] with no hype words or all-caps."
4. "Turn this pain point into 12 subject lines: [paste pain]. Sound like a human."
5. "Create 10 proof-based subject lines referencing [result] without inventing numbers."
6. "Write 10 urgency subject lines for a [date] deadline. No false scarcity."
7. "Score these 8 subject lines 1-10 for curiosity and clarity, then improve the top 2: [paste]."
8. "Give me 10 subject lines under 40 characters for a mobile-first list about [topic]."
9. "Write A/B pairs for each of these 5 angles: [paste angles]. One short, one specific."
10. "Draft 20 preview text lines that complete, not repeat, these subjects: [paste]."

## 4. Pre-send checklist

- Under 55 characters so mobile does not cut it off.
- No spam triggers: FREE!!!, ACT NOW, 100% GUARANTEED.
- The preview text adds new information, never repeats the subject.
- The promise matches the first line of the email body.
- One clear angle per send. Never mix urgency and proof.
