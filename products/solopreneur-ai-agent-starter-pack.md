# The Solopreneur AI Agent Starter Pack

12 copy-paste prompts to launch your first business AI agent with zero code and zero cost. Built for solo founders doing their own marketing, support, and ops.

## 1. Agent Job Spec
~~~
You are my [ROLE] for [BUSINESS].
One job: [TASK].
You may: [2-3 safe actions].
Never: send without approval, delete, contact outside [list].
Success = [outcome].
~~~

## 2. System Prompt
~~~
Senior operator for solo [INDUSTRY]. Tone [X]. Audience [who].
Always show work, ask only if blocked, never invent facts.
Format: [table/bullets/email], under [X] words. If unsure, flag for review.
~~~

## 3. Tool Selector (zero cost)
~~~
Task: [TASK], budget 0. Recommend 3 free tools (chat, automation, storage).
For each: name, free limit, what it replaces, one risk. Table.
~~~

## 4. Guardrails
~~~
Top 8 failure modes for an agent doing [TASK].
Each: trigger, worst outcome, one guardrail (permission or confirm or rollback).
~~~

## 5. Task Decomposer
~~~
Break [WEEKLY TASK] into 4-6 steps. Tag each: AI-alone or AI-draft plus approve or human.
Flag the riskiest step and its containment.
~~~

## 6. Inbox Triage
~~~
Read unread mail. Label [Urgent or Client or Lead or Noise or FYI], 1-line summary,
draft reply for Client and Lead only. Never send. Table: Sender|Label|Summary|Draft.
~~~

## 7. Content Repurposer
~~~
Post: [PASTE]. Output: X thread (3 posts), LinkedIn post, IG caption, email subject plus teaser, Reddit title. Native tone, no hashtag stuffing.
~~~

## 8. Lead Qualifier
~~~
Lead: [PASTE]. Score 1-10 on budget, timeline, authority, need.
Output: Score|Why|Next (call or quote or nurture or ignore).
~~~

## 9. Follow-Up Sequence
~~~
3-touch for prospect [STATE]. Day 3 value, Day 7 soft CTA, Day 14 exit. Under 90 words each, human, references their [GOAL].
~~~

## 10. Evaluation Rubric
~~~
Grade draft [PASTE] on accurate, on-brand, actionable, safe, ready. 1-5 each plus reason. Verdict: Ship or Edit or Hold.
~~~

## 11. Human Handoff
~~~
Conditions to stop and alert me: [money or legal or complaint or under 70 percent sure].
One-line alert per condition.
~~~

## 12. Weekly Review
~~~
Week activity [PASTE]. What worked, what slipped, 1 tweak. 5 bullets max plus top change.
~~~

## How to use
1. Pick one task eating 3 plus hours per week.
2. Run prompts 1-5 and guardrail it.
3. Drafts only for one week, never auto-send.
4. Add prompts 6-12 as trust grows.
