# The Solopreneur's AI Output Fact-Check Checklist

Ship AI-generated work fast without shipping AI mistakes. A 21-point pre-publish verification system built for solo founders who have no editor, no legal team, and no time to waste.

## What's inside
- A claim-risk triage table (know what can hurt you)
- The 21-point pre-publish checklist
- 3 copy-paste verification prompts
- A source-of-truth log template
- A 10-minute verification sprint

---

## 1. Why this exists
AI drafts are fluent, not factual. A single invented statistic, wrong price, or fabricated quote in a sales page, newsletter, or client deliverable costs more trust than a week of slow writing. This pack turns verification into a 10-minute routine instead of an anxious re-read.

---

## 2. Claim-Risk Triage (do this before anything else)
| Risk tier | Examples | Required action |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| High | Prices, dates, legal/medical/financial claims, named quotes, statistics | Verify against a primary source; log it; never publish on model memory alone |
| Medium | Tool features, plan limits, competitor facts, version numbers | Open the vendor's own docs/page and confirm the current value |
| Low | Tone, examples, generic advice, formatting | Spot-check for plausibility only |

Rule: every High claim gets a source. No source, no publish.

## 3. The 21-Point Pre-Publish Checklist
| # | Check | Pass when |
|---|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Every number appears in a source | Linked in the log |
| 2 | Prices match the live page | Checked today |
| 3 | Dates and "as of" labels are current | Within 30 days |
| 4 | Named quotes are verifiable | Original transcript/link found |
| 5 | No invented book, study, or paper | Title + author confirmed |
| 6 | Tool/feature names are real | Vendor docs confirm |
| 7 | Free-tier limits still accurate | Pricing page checked |
| 8 | No "everyone knows" claims unsourced | Source or deleted |
| 9 | Stats keep their original scope | Sample, year, geography intact |
| 10 | Percentages are not silently rounded up | Recomputed |
| 11 | Comparisons are like-for-like | Same units/timeframe |
| 12 | Links resolve (no 404) | Clicked all |
| 13 | Links point to the intended domain | No typo-squats |
| 14 | Examples are labelled as examples | Not presented as case studies |
| 15 | Client names/results are permissioned | Written approval on file |
| 16 | No hidden AI-generated testimonials | Removed |
| 17 | Disclosure line present where required | Added to footer |
| 18 | Headline matches body claims | No overpromise |
| 19 | CTA promises only what is delivered | Verified |
| 20 | Spelling of names/brands checked | Against official site |
| 21 | Final read-aloud for tone | Sounds like you, not a bot |

## 4. Three Verification Prompts
**A. Claim extractor**
> "List every factual claim in the text below that could be checked against an outside source. For each: the exact sentence, a risk tier (High/Medium/Low), and the type of source that would confirm it. Do not verify anything. Text: [PASTE]"

**B. Adversarial reviewer**
> "Act as a skeptical fact-checker. For each High-risk claim below, state what evidence would disprove it and where a reader would look for that evidence. Flag anything that sounds plausible but is likely model-generated. Claims: [PASTE]"

**C. Scope guard**
> "Rewrite the following paragraph so that every claim is strictly bounded by what the cited source actually supports. Remove or hedge anything beyond the source. Keep the original voice. Paragraph: [PASTE] Source: [PASTE]"

Never paste the whole draft into one prompt and ask "is this correct?" — models will agree with themselves. Extract first, verify outside the model, then rewrite.

---

## 5. Source-of-Truth Log (copy into your notes)
```
DATE:        [YYYY-MM-DD]
ASSET:       [newsletter issue / landing page / client doc]
CLAIM:       [exact sentence]
TIER:        [High / Medium / Low]
SOURCE:      [URL or document]
CHECKED:     [date]
VERIFIED BY: [you]
NOTE:        [any caveat]
```
Keep one line per High claim. It takes 30 seconds and turns a dispute into a non-event.

---

## 6. The 10-Minute Verification Sprint
| Minute | Task |
|--------|------|
| 0-2 | Run Prompt A, paste claims into the triage table |
| 2-6 | Open sources for every High claim, log them |
| 6-8 | Run Prompt B on the remaining Medium claims |
| 8-9 | Click every link, check headline vs body |
| 9-10 | Add disclosure line, read the last paragraph aloud |

If a High claim cannot be verified in 6 minutes, delete it. Speed is the point.
