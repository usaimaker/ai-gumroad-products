## 2. Runway Calculator

Runway (months) = Cash today / Monthly burn

| Line item | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Cash in bank today | $ |
| Avg monthly fixed costs | $ |
| Avg monthly variable costs | $ |
| Monthly burn (fixed + variable) | $ |
| Big bills due in 90 days | $ |
| Adjusted cash (cash - big bills) | $ |
| RUNWAY = adjusted cash / burn | months |

Read it: under 3 months = cut costs this week. 3-6 months = start selling now. 6+ months = safe to invest.

## 3. Three Scenarios

| Scenario | Assumption | Action |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Base | Clients pay on time, no new sales | Hold course; update grid weekly |
| Down 30% | Two invoices slip 30 days | Cut variable costs 20%, pause ads, chase receivables on day 1 |
| Up 30% | One retainer closes | Keep tax aside, bank surplus, delay lifestyle inflation |

## 4. 10 Copy-Paste AI Prompts

1. "Here are my last 6 months of income and expenses: [paste]. Split into fixed vs variable, compute average monthly burn and runway in months. Show the math."
2. "Act as a CFO. I have $[cash] and burn $[burn]/month. List 10 specific cuts worth $[target]/month, ranked by ease and impact."
3. "Turn these invoices [paste] into a 13-week receipt forecast table. Mark each likely / at-risk / unlikely with a one-line reason."
4. "Write a polite but firm 3-email late-payment sequence for a client 14, 30, and 45 days overdue."
5. "Given this 13-week grid [paste], flag the first week cash goes negative and suggest three fixes with dollar amounts."
6. "Create a down-30% stress test for my numbers [paste]. Show closing cash per week and the exact week I must act."
7. "Draft a one-page cash update for my accountant: opening, receipts, costs, tax set-aside, runway."
8. "I want to hire at $[cost]/month. Using my runway of [n] months, tell me yes or no and what buffer I must keep."
9. "Rewrite this pricing sentence so it anchors on value, not hours: [paste]."
10. "Build me a weekly 15-minute cash routine: exact steps, what to check, and a one-line decision at the end."

## 5. The 15-Minute Weekly Cash Routine

1. Monday: log last week's actual receipts and costs in the grid.
2. Update runway using the calculator table.
3. Chase any invoice over 7 days late (use prompt 4).
4. Move the tax set-aside to a separate account.
5. Read the scenario table: are you Base, Down, or Up? Write one action.

## 6. Red Flags - Act Immediately

| Signal | Why it matters | Fix today |
|--------|---------------|-----------|
| Runway under 3 months | One quarter of oxygen left | Cut variable costs, offer annual prepay discount |
| One client over 40% of receipts | Single point of failure | Pitch 3 new leads this week |
| Tax column empty | Surprise bill later | Auto-transfer 25% on receipt |
| Closing cash negative any week | Rent or payroll will miss | Delay one cost or collect one invoice early |
