# The Solopreneur's AI Invoice & Late-Payment Follow-Up Kit

Late payment is the #1 cash-flow killer for solo businesses. This kit gives you the fields, escalation calendar, and copy-paste scripts that get you paid without awkwardness.

## 1. Invoice Fields (miss one, wait weeks)
| Field | Put this |
|---|---|
| Number / dates | INV-2026-001, issue + due date |
| Terms | Net 14 on the invoice AND contract |
| Legal names | Yours + client legal name, AP email |
| Line items | Qty x rate, subtotal, tax, total |
| Payment link | Stripe/Wise/PayPal + accepted methods |
| Late fee | 1.5%/month after due date |
| PO number | If the client requires one |

Rule: take a 30-50% deposit up front from every new client or project over $1,000.

## 2. Terms Cheat Sheet
| Term | Use when |
|---|---|
| Due on receipt | Jobs under $500 |
| Net 7 | New clients |
| Net 14 | Standard services (best default) |
| Net 30 | Established corporate clients only |
| 50/50 | Projects over $1,000 |
| 30/40/30 | Projects over $5,000 |

## 3. Escalation Calendar
| Day | Tone | Channel | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| -3 | Friendly | Email | "Anything you need?" |
| 0 | Polite | Email | Amount + link |
| +7 | Friendly | Email | Assume oversight, resend |
| +14 | Firm | Email | Cite fee, ask exact date |
| +14 | Direct | Phone | Spoken commitment |
| +30 | Formal | Email+PDF | Stop work, apply fee |
| +60 | Legal | Certified mail | Final notice |

80% of late invoices resolve with 1-2 reminders in the first 7 days.

## 4. Copy-Paste Scripts (edit brackets only)
Day -3 (Subject: Quick check on [NUMBER]): "Hi [Name] - invoice [NUMBER] for [AMOUNT] is due [DATE]. All good? Happy to resend or add a PO number."
Day 0 (Subject: [NUMBER] due today): "Hi [Name] - invoice [NUMBER] for [AMOUNT] is due today. Pay here: [LINK]. If already sent, ignore this."
Day 7 (Subject: [NUMBER] - 7 days overdue): "Hi [Name] - invoice [NUMBER] for [AMOUNT], due [DATE], may have slipped through. Link: [LINK]. Please confirm the expected payment date."
Day 14 (Subject: [NUMBER] - action needed): "Hi [Name] - invoice [NUMBER] is 14 days past due. Per our terms a 1.5% late fee applies; new total [AMOUNT]. Confirm a payment date today or tell me the billing issue."
Day 30 (Subject: Final notice: [NUMBER]): "Hi [Name] - invoice [NUMBER] is 30 days overdue. All work is paused until payment clears. Reply by [DATE] with a firm date."
Phone: "I am calling about invoice [NUMBER] for [AMOUNT]. I just need a date I can plan around - can you give me one?"
Scope: "The extra work is outside the agreed deliverables. Additional fee: [AMOUNT]. Reply approved and I will send a change order; I will not start before then."

## 5. Why Clients Do Not Pay
| Reason | Signal | Your line |
|---|---|---|
| Oversight | First delay | "Attached again - here is the link." |
| Payment cycle | "We pay on the 15th" | "What is your next payment run?" |
| Approval | "Need sign-off" | "Who should I copy for approval?" |
| Dispute | Vague excuses | "Any deliverable concern I can fix?" |
| Cash flow | "Next month" | "40% now, 30% in 30d, 30% in 60d?" |
| Avoidance | Went silent | Call; formal notice; stop work. |

## 6. Formulas
Late fee = total x 1.5% x months overdue. $2,000 x 1.5% x 1 = $30. Only charge fees stated before work began; check local caps.
Deposit = 30-50% upfront over $1,000: "Deposit - 50% of project total", then "Final balance" on delivery.

## 9. Setup in 30 Minutes
1. Add the fields to your invoice template (10 min).
2. Add the late-fee clause to contract + footer (5 min).
3. Save scripts in notes (5 min).
4. Set reminders at -3, 0, +7, +14, +30 (5 min).
5. Start the tracker with 5 invoices (5 min).

## 10. Anti-Awkwardness Rules
Never apologize for asking to be paid. Restate number, amount, due date; attach the invoice. Escalate on schedule, not emotion. Stop new work when late. After two ignored emails, call and get a spoken date.
