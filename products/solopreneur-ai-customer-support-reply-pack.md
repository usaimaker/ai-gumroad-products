# The Solopreneur's AI Customer Support Reply Pack

You are the whole support team. This pack gives you 15 paste-ready replies plus the rules and AI prompts to adapt them to any product - SaaS, store, or service. Reply in minutes, sound human, protect your time.

## The 5 Rules (so replies never sound like a bot)
| # | Rule | Why it works |
|---|------|--------------|
| 1 | Reply within 24h, even if it is "looking into it, update by Thursday" | Silence feels like being ignored; a time beats a vague promise |
| 2 | Apologize once | Three "sorries" reads as panic |
| 3 | Name the fix AND a date | "I have refunded you; banks take 3-10 days" is a fix; "I will look into it" is not |
| 4 | Offer a choice when you can | Refund or replacement turns an angry buyer into a decider |
| 5 | Keep it under 100 words | Long replies look defensive |

## 15 Copy-Paste Reply Templates
Replace {brackets}. Save these as snippets in Gmail, Help Scout, or a text expander.

| # | Scenario | Reply |
|---|----------|-------|
| 1 | Bug report | Hi {name}, thanks for flagging this and sorry for the trouble. I can reproduce it: {one-line}. A fix ships {date}; until then, try {workaround}. I will reply here when it is live. |
| 2 | Cannot reproduce | Hi {name}, I tried {steps} on {version} and it works on my side, so something in your setup differs. Could you send {OS/browser/version} plus a screenshot of {screen}? That usually pins it down fast. |

| 5 | Feature you will not build | Hi {name}, good idea but it is not on the roadmap because {honest reason}. Meanwhile, {integration/alternative} gets you most of the way. |
| 6 | Feature you might build | Hi {name}, added your use case to the list. I cannot promise a date, but I will email if it ships. |
| 7 | Outage | Hi {name}, yes - {service} has been down for some users since {time}. Cause: {short cause}. Next update by {time}. Sorry for the disruption. |
| 8 | Cancellation | Hi {name}, done: cancelled, no further charges. You keep access until {date}. If you return, your data is held {days} days. |
| 9 | Angry customer | Hi {name}, I get why you are frustrated and I am sorry we let you down. Right now I am {action}. You will hear from me by {time}. |
| 10 | Negative public review | Hi {name}, thank you for the honest feedback and sorry about {issue}. That is not the experience we want. I have messaged you so we can make it right. |
| 11 | How-to question | Hey {name} - here is the path: {2-3 steps}. Full walkthrough: {doc link}. If that does not match, reply with a screenshot. |
| 12 | Billing or change plan | Hi {name}, manage your plan here: {link}. If you are cancelling because something broke, tell me what went wrong - I would rather fix it. |
| 13 | Upset about price | Hi {name}, fair - pricing reflects {value}. If it helps, {lower tier / annual discount} drops it to {amount}. Want me to switch you? |
| 14 | Thank-you or fan | Hi {name}, this made my day - thank you! If you ever want to share your story, I would love to feature you. No pressure. |
| 15 | Win-back (gone 60+ days) | Hi {name}, you have been missed. I have added {new feature / fix} since you left. Want a fresh link to try again? |

## Build Your Reply Library in 30 Minutes
1. Copy the 15 replies into a notes doc.
2. Replace every {bracket} with your product's real values (refund window, links, dates).
3. Save each as a snippet: Gmail "Templates", Help Scout "Saved Replies", or a text expander (e.g. type /bug).
4. Add one doc link to your most-asked how-to (cuts repeat tickets).
5. Set two inbox blocks (e.g. 9am + 4pm) and close it otherwise.

## 5 AI Prompts to Adapt Any Template
1. `Rewrite reply 3 for a subscription product with a 14-day no-questions refund. Keep under 80 words.`
2. `Make reply 9 warmer but still under 100 words for a SaaS audience.`
3. `Turn my FAQ into 3 canned replies like the table, one per top question.`
4. `Write a polite version of reply 4 that offers a 1-month credit instead of a refund.`
5. `Generate 5 more replies for: late payment, duplicate charge, access link not working, bulk order, partnership request.`

## Anti-Robot Tone Checklist (before you hit send)
- [ ] Used their name
- [ ] One apology max
- [ ] Concrete fix plus date stated
- [ ] A choice offered if possible
- [ ] Under 100 words
- [ ] No blame on the customer
