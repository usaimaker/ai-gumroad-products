# The Solopreneur's AI Faceless Video Script Pack

Faceless short-form video (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels) is the single lowest-effort way for a solopreneur to build an audience and a second income stream in 2026. No face, no camera, no editing degree just a script, a free text-to-speech voice, and a stock clip. This pack gives you 15 copy-paste scripts plus the exact system to batch 30 videos in one afternoon.

## Why faceless works for solopreneurs
- Zero on-camera risk: stay anonymous, stay consistent.
- Evergreen: a 30-second clip earns for months.
- Cheap to make: free TTS plus free stock equals $0 per video.
- Repurposable: one script becomes Shorts plus TikTok plus Reels plus a blog snippet.

## The 3-part hook formula (use every time)
| Hook type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Curiosity gap | You are doing X wrong if | You are pricing your offer wrong if you charge by the hour. |
| Identity | Stop doing X if you are a Y | Stop posting daily if you are a solo founder. |
| Result | How I got X without Y | How I got 10k views without showing my face. |
