# The Solopreneur's AI Social Proof & Testimonial Collection Kit

Your product is great. Nobody knows it because you have three testimonials from your mom and a college roommate. This kit fixes that in one focused week: exactly when to ask, what to ask, and how to turn a thumbs-up DM into a quote that sells.

## Why this works
- 92% of buyers read reviews before purchasing; a single specific result beats ten vague "great service" lines.
- Asking at the right moment (right after a win) lifts response rates from ~8% to ~40%.
- AI lets you draft the ask, polish the reply, and build a case study without hiring a copywriter.

## The 8 best moments to ask (and what to request)

| # | Trigger moment | Channel | Ask for |
|---|---------------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Client hits a milestone you helped with | Email / DM | One-sentence result + metric |
| 2 | Support ticket closed with a thank you | Email | Short quote about support speed |
| 3 | Course module completed | In-app / Email | What changed for them |
| 4 | Renewal / repeat purchase | Email | Why they came back |
| 5 | Positive social comment | DM | Permission to feature + 1 line |
| 6 | Free trial converts to paid | Email | What made them upgrade |
| 7 | After a live call / workshop | Email within 24h | Takeaway that mattered |
| 8 | Unsolicited praise arrives | DM immediately | Can we use this publicly? |

Rule: ask within 24 hours of the win. Memory fades fast; specifics do not.

## Fill-in testimonial intake (send this form)

1. What were you struggling with before working with us?
2. What result did you get? (include a number if you have one)
3. What specifically surprised you or made it easy?
4. Would you recommend us to a fellow role? Why?
5. Can we use your full name, photo, and company? (Y/N)
6. Tag a friend who has the same problem.

## 5 copy-paste AI prompts

Prompt 1 - Polish a raw quote:
```
Take this raw customer message and rewrite it as a tight, 2-sentence testimonial that names a specific result. Keep their voice, remove filler, add one metric if implied. Raw: "[paste]"
```

Prompt 2 - Generate a case study outline:
```
From this customer outcome: "[paste result + metric]", build a one-page case study: headline, problem, action taken, result, quote, lesson. Plain English, no fluff.
```

Prompt 3 - Write the ask email:
```
Write a 90-word email asking a happy client for a testimonial. Context: they just [milestone]. Tone warm, specific, low-pressure. Include a one-click intake link.
```

Prompt 4 - UGC permission request:
```
Draft a 40-word Instagram or TikTok DM asking a customer to post a photo using our product and tag us, in exchange for a feature. Friendly, not salesy.
```

Prompt 5 - Objection-busting quote hunt:
```
List 5 testimonial angles that would overcome a buyer's top doubt: "[doubt]". For each, the exact question to ask the customer who can prove it.
```

## 7-day sprint

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | List your 20 happiest past clients; pick the 8 from the trigger table |
| 2 | Send Prompt 3 emails to all 8 |
| 3 | Post your two review links in your bio + email signature |
| 4 | Convert replies with Prompt 1; request permission (intake) |
| 5 | Build 2 case studies with Prompt 2 |
| 6 | Publish a testimonials page; embed 5 quotes + 1 case study |
| 7 | DM 10 recent happy customers for UGC (Prompt 4) |

## Anti-cringe checklist
- Specific beats polite. "Saved me 6 hours a week" beats "very professional."
- Real name + photo beats anonymous. Anonymous quotes convert about 3x worse.
- One metric beats five adjectives.
- Ask for permission before publishing. Always.
- Never edit a quote to change its meaning.
