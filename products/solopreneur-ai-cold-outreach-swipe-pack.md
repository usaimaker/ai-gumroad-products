## The 5-Step Follow-Up Sequence (never send once and quit)
1. Day 0 - Value DM/email (prompt 1 or 3).
2. Day 3 - Proof follow-up (prompt 2 or 4).
3. Day 7 - Different angle or case (prompt 11 plus 12).
4. Day 14 - Break-up (prompt 5 or 15).
5. Day 30 - New insight win-back (prompt 15).

Rule: same prospect, max 5 touches, then stop for 60 days.

## 3 Rules That Keep You Out Of Spam
- One clear CTA per message. Never two asks.
- Reference something specific about THEM in line 1.
- Send from a warmed-up domain: 20-30 sends per day max per inbox.
