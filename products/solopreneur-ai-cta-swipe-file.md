# The Solopreneur's AI CTA Swipe File

40 fill-in calls to action for buttons, emails, DMs, posts, pop-ups and video - plus the exact AI prompts to write more in your own voice.

## Use it in 3 minutes
1. Pick the surface (button / email / DM / post / pop-up / video).
2. Fill the brackets with the outcome, never the feature.
3. Add one friction-killer line under the CTA (see Micro-copy).

## The 6 jobs a CTA can do
| Job | Use when | Verb | Fill-in example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capture an email | reader is curious, not ready | Send | "Send me the [checklist name]" |
| Book a call | high-ticket service | Grab | "Grab a free 15-min [outcome] audit" |
| Start a trial | tool / SaaS | Try | "Try it free for [N] days" |
| Buy now | warm traffic, clear price | Get | "Get the [product] for $[price]" |
| Download | lead magnet | Download | "Download the [N]-point [topic] checklist" |
| Reply / DM | social, conversation first | Reply | "Reply [word] and I'll send it" |

## Micro-copy that removes friction
| Placement | Weak | Stronger |
|---|---|---|
| Under button | "Submit" | "Free. No card. Takes 30 seconds." |
| Under button | "Click here" | "Instant download - 1 page, printable." |
| Email field | "Newsletter signup" | "One practical email a week. Unsubscribe anytime." |
| Above button | "Free guide" | "Write your first [email] in 10 minutes." |
| Price CTA | "Buy now" | "Get [product] - $[price], lifetime access." |
| DM close | "Let me know" | "Reply yes and I'll send it right over." |

## 7-day A/B sprint
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Pick one surface. Write version A (outcome) and B (curiosity). |
| 2 | Put A and B live, split traffic 50/50. Same button colour. |
| 3 | Do not touch anything. Let data accumulate. |
| 4 | Add a friction-killer line under both. Keep the split. |
| 5 | Read clicks / sends. Keep the winner, kill the loser. |
| 6 | Write a new challenger against the winner using prompt 1. |
| 7 | Log winning wording in your swipe file. Repeat with the next surface. |

## Pre-publish checklist
- Starts with a verb; under 8 words for buttons.
- Names the outcome or the deliverable, not the format.
- One CTA per screen - no competing asks.
- Friction-killer line directly beneath it.
- Brackets replaced; no placeholder text left.
- Tested on mobile where the button is actually visible.
