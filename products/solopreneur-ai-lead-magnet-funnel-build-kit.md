# The Solopreneur's AI Lead Magnet Funnel Build Kit

Turn one freebie into a leak-proof pipeline from opt-in to nurture in a single afternoon. No design skills, no paid tools, no agency.

## 1. Pick the right format

Match the reader's mindset to the format. One freebie, one promise.

| Format | Best when the reader... | Build time | 2026 opt-in rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quiz | Wants an answer about themselves | 3-6 hrs | ~40 percent (highest) |
| Checklist | Wants a fast win with zero effort | 2 min | High |
| Template | Hates the blank page | 3 min | High |
| Swipe file | Wants real examples to copy | 5 min | Medium-High |
| Mini-course | Needs a multi-step fix | 10 min | Medium |

Rule: choose ONE. A funnel with two lead magnets splits attention and leaks subscribers.

## 2. Form-field reduction cheat

Every extra field costs opt-ins. Cut to the bone.

| Field | Keep? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Email | YES | Non-negotiable |
| First name | Optional | Lets you personalize; costs about 5 percent opt-ins |
| Company | NO | You are not HR |
| Phone | NO | Instant drop-off |
| Password | NO | Use double opt-in email instead |

Formula: required fields = 1 (email). Add a name only if you will actually use it in email 1.

## 3. The 5-step build sequence

1. Write the freebie around ONE specific outcome (a number, a time, or a named result).
2. Build the opt-in form with a single field; connect it to your ESP (Kit, Beehiiv, or Substack).
3. Set auto-delivery so the ESP emails the file within the hour of signup.
4. Tag the subscriber by the freebie they grabbed; route them into the matching nurture sequence.
5. Point the final nurture email to your paid offer or a booking link.

## 5. Six copy-paste AI prompts

1. "My niche is [X]. My reader's number one pain is [Y]. Give me 5 lead magnet titles, each promising a specific outcome with a number or a time."
2. "Turn this freebie idea [Z] into a one-page checklist with 8 steps and one sentence of context per step."
3. "Write the 3-email nurture sequence for a lead magnet about [topic]: email 1 delivers and sets expectations, email 2 a quick win, email 3 a soft pitch to [offer]."
4. "My opt-in page converts at [rate] percent. List 10 reasons it leaks signups and rank them by impact."
5. "Draft the exit-intent offer for my [page type] page: a 20-word promise that turns leavers into subscribers."
6. "Write 5 subject lines for the delivery email that get it opened above 60 percent."

## 6. Conversion benchmark cheat

| Metric | Healthy | Leaking |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page opt-in | 25-40 percent | under 15 percent |
| Delivery open rate | over 60 percent | under 40 percent |
| Nurture click rate | 5-12 percent | under 2 percent |
| Funnel to call | 2-5 percent | under 1 percent |

If a number sits in the leaking column, fix that step before you build anything new.

## 7. Anti-leak checklist

- [ ] One lead magnet, one promise
- [ ] Email is the only required field
- [ ] File delivered within 60 minutes
- [ ] Subscriber tagged before nurture starts
- [ ] Every nurture email has one clear next step
- [ ] Final email links to the paid offer
