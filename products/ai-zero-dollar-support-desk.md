# The Zero-Dollar Support Desk: A Solo Founder Free Customer-Support Stack for 2026

Run real customer support on a real budget of zero dollars. This kit maps the free tiers that actually hold, the four features vendors always paywall, and a 60-minute setup you can finish this afternoon.

## Why a free support desk is a real plan in 2026

Most founders think good support requires a paid Zendesk seat. It does not. In 2026 several vendors give away the core of their product forever, because their business model is to charge you the moment your team starts depending on it. That is not a trap to fear. It is a timeline you can plan around.

The goal here is not to recreate an enterprise help center. It is to answer three questions honestly: where do customers reach you, how fast do you reply, and what happens to a conversation after the first message. A free stack answers all three well enough to serve your first few hundred customers.

## The free tiers that actually hold

- Tawk.to is free forever with unlimited agents, unlimited conversations, and unlimited chat history. The core covers live chat, a basic ticketing system, a knowledge base, and a simple CRM. It shows real-time visitor monitoring and lets you save canned shortcuts for fast replies. Branding removal costs 29 dollars per month. AI Assist and hired live agents are optional paid add-ons.
- Zoho Desk is free for up to 3 agents with email ticketing, a help center, and a mobile app. It lacks automation, SLAs, and live chat on the free plan. Paid tiers run roughly 7 to 40 dollars per agent per month.
- Crisp gives a permanent free plan with 2 seats and unlimited conversations across a multichannel inbox that pulls in email, Messenger, Instagram, and Telegram. Pricing is flat per workspace rather than per agent.
- Freshdesk free supports up to 10 agents with email and social channels plus a knowledge base and a shared queue. Automation, SLA policies, and AI sit in paid plans starting near 15 dollars per agent per month.
- HubSpot Service Hub Free bundles free chat and ticketing with a free CRM for up to 2 users, which is the natural pick if you already run HubSpot for sales.
- For open-source control, FreeScout and osTicket are self-hosted with no agent limits, and Chatwoot offers a self-hosted build or a small cloud free tier with 20 plus channels including WhatsApp.

## The four features vendors always paywall

- Seat and volume caps. Zoho Desk stops at 3 agents and Crisp at 2, while Tawk.to and Freshdesk impose no meaningful seat limit. If more than three people ever answer, your shortlist narrows fast.
- Automation. Routing rules, macros, workflow triggers, and auto-escalation almost always live in paid tiers. Free plans give you a shared queue and little else.
- AI. AI-drafted replies and autonomous agents are the most aggressively gated feature of all. Vendors typically meter AI at roughly 0.50 to 1.00 dollar per resolved conversation, or sell it as a separate credit pack.
- Reporting. Free tiers show raw ticket counts. CSAT surveys, SLA compliance, and agent performance dashboards land in mid tiers, which means free teams often cannot answer the simplest question: what is our first response time.

## Collect feedback without paying

A support desk captures problems. A feedback tool tells you what to fix next, and several are free enough to run in production.

- Formbricks is open source and AGPL licensed. Self-hosting is free with zero response limits and full data ownership. Its EU cloud free tier ships a few hundred to a couple thousand responses per month depending on the plan you read, so confirm the current number on the vendor page before relying on it.
- Tally offers unlimited forms and unlimited submissions on a fair-use guideline near 50,000 per month, with conditional logic and file uploads. It has no built-in AI analysis, so treat it as a collector, not an analyzer.
- Google Forms is completely free with unlimited forms and responses and a clean Sheets export. It is the right call for internal or low-design surveys.
- Typeform free caps at 10 responses per month, which is fine for testing a single conversational form but not for real volume.
- FeedSense gives 100 feedback submissions per month with built-in AI sentiment and auto-categorization, a useful way to see what AI analysis feels like before you pay.

## When free stops being free

Watch for four upgrade triggers. First, you hit a seat or volume cap more than once a quarter. Second, agents spend more than five hours a week on manual routing and repetitive replies that automation could handle. Third, you cannot answer basic metrics because reporting is paywalled. Fourth, returning customers keep re-explaining their issue because your tool retains no useful history.

The hidden cost of a free plan is rarely the software. It is the labor. A two-person team spending 45 minutes a day triaging and tagging is roughly 30 hours a month. At a 25 dollar loaded hourly cost, that is about 750 dollars a month in work to save 30 dollars in fees. Upgrade when two of the triggers show up together, not after a year of backlog.

## How to use

1. Pick your front door. Install Tawk.to or HubSpot chat on your site today. Both are live in 15 to 30 minutes with a single embed snippet. Start with one channel only.
2. Write ten canned replies. Cover the questions you answer most: pricing, shipping, refunds, account access, and onboarding. Save them as shortcuts so replies stay consistent and fast.
3. Stand up a one-page help center. Use the knowledge base inside your chosen tool, or a single Google Doc linked in the widget. Answer the same ten questions in long form.
4. Add a feedback loop. Drop a Tally or Formbricks link in your thank-you email and your chat widget. Aim to read every response weekly.
5. Set a reply promise and keep it. Even a solo founder can commit to responding within one business day. State it in the widget so customers know what to expect.
6. Review every Monday for 15 minutes. Read new feedback, update two canned replies, and check whether any upgrade trigger fired this week.
7. Re-check free-tier limits quarterly. Vendors change caps without notice. Spend 10 minutes on each pricing page and adjust before a launch spikes your volume.
