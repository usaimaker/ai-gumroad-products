# The Solopreneur's AI Quiz Lead Magnet Builder

Quizzes are the number-one lead magnet of 2026 - averaging about 40 percent start-to-lead conversion versus 5 to 15 percent for PDFs and checklists. Buyers love them because the result is personal, instantly useful, and shareable. This kit lets you ship a buyer-segmenting quiz in one afternoon, using free AI for every step.

## Why a Quiz Beats a PDF
- Higher opt-in: a tailored result is worth an email address.
- Built-in segmentation: the answer someone picks tells you what to sell them.
- Self-qualifying: tire-kickers self-select out; buyers raise their hand.
- Evergreen: one quiz keeps converting long after you build it.

## Step 1 - Pick Your Quiz Type
| Type | What it does | Best for |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Personality | sorts people into named archetypes | coaches, communities, brands |
| Score | tallies points into tiers (low/mid/high) | SaaS, finance, productivity |
| Assessment | diagnoses a problem's severity | health, ops, marketing audits |
| Diagnostic | points to the single right next step | services, courses, tools |

## Step 2 - Build in 5 Moves
| # | Action | AI helps with | Time |
|---|--------|---------------|------|
| 1 | Name the outcome | title plus 3 result buckets | 10 min |
| 2 | Write 6 to 8 questions | question bank below | 20 min |
| 3 | Map answers to buckets | scoring logic | 15 min |
| 4 | Draft result copy | per-bucket promise plus CTA | 20 min |
| 5 | Wire opt-in and email | form plus 3-email nurture | 25 min |

## Step 3 - The Question Bank (copy-paste)
| Q# | Question | Type | Bucket logic |
|----|----------|------|--------------|

## Step 4 - Result Buckets That Sell
| Bucket | Promise | Soft CTA |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Starter | You are two tweaks from traction | free mini-guide |
| Builder | You have the base, here is the gap | paid course |
| Scaler | You are ready to systematize | 1:1 offer |
| Stuck | Let us find the root cause | diagnostic call |

## Step 5 - 10 AI Prompts to Generate the Whole Quiz
| # | Paste this into any AI |
|---|------------------------|
| 1 | Design a personality or score quiz for my niche with 3 result buckets and a clear promise for each. |
| 2 | Write 8 multiple-choice questions that segment my audience by readiness to buy. |
| 3 | Map these answers to 3 buckets and show the scoring math. |
| 4 | For each bucket, write a 60-word result with a specific win and a soft CTA. |
| 5 | Write the opt-in form copy: headline, 3 bullet benefits, button text. |
| 6 | Draft email 1 (instant result plus expectation), email 2 (quick win), email 3 (soft pitch). |
| 7 | Give me 5 shareable result headlines a user would post to social. |
| 8 | Write the quiz landing page: H1, subhead, 3 proof points, CTA. |
| 9 | List 10 places to promote this quiz for free this week. |
| 10 | Build a 7-day launch checklist to drive the first 500 leads. |

## Step 6 - Deliver and Nurture
- Email 1 (within 1 hour): deliver the result, set expectations, no pitch.
- Email 2 (day 2): one quick win tied to their bucket.
- Email 3 (day 4): soft pitch matching their bucket's CTA.
- Tag every subscriber by bucket so future sends stay relevant.
