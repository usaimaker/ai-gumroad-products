## The 8-Column Calendar
Copy this grid into Google Sheets (freeze row 1, color-code by platform):

| Date | Platform | Post Type | Pillar | Hook (first 7 words) | Body / Caption | Asset | Status / CTA |
|------|----------|-----------|--------|----------------------|----------------|-------|--------------|
| Mon Jun 22 | Instagram | Carousel | Educate | 3 mistakes quietly killing your reach | Your caption here | /assets/jun22.png | Scheduled |
| Tue Jun 23 | LinkedIn | Text | Founder story | I almost quit at month 3 here is why | Your caption here | none | Draft |

Columns explained:
- Date = publish day, not create day
- Platform = one row per platform per post
- Post Type = image, carousel, short video, story, text, link
- Pillar = which of your 4-5 content pillars this supports
- Hook = first 7 words; they decide if anyone keeps reading
- Body = the actual caption or post copy
- Asset = link to the visual or source file
- Status / CTA = draft to scheduled to published, plus the call to action

## The 5-Post Weekly Mix
Algorithms in 2026 reward format variety. Use this ratio:
- Educate 40 percent - a tip, framework, or how-to that earns the save
- Social proof 20 percent - a customer quote, review screenshot, or transformation
- Entertain 20 percent - a meme, behind-the-scenes clip, or trend hijack that earns the share
- Behind-the-scenes 15 percent - owner POV or process clip that earns the follow
- Promote 5 percent - one direct ask per week: your offer, booking link, or lead magnet

## 4-Week Execution Rhythm
- Day 1 - Plan: block 60 minutes, fill the next 30 days using the 5-post mix
- Day 2 - Batch: create all assets in one session (cuts production time about 60 percent)
- Day 3 - Schedule: drop everything into Buffer or Meta Business Suite
- Daily - React: leave 1-2 open slots per week for trends, wins, or news

## 2026 Posting Cadence by Platform
- LinkedIn: 3-5 posts per week, Mon-Thu 8-10am
- Instagram: 4-5 posts per week plus daily Stories
- X: 5-10 posts per week
- TikTok: 4-7 short videos per week
- Facebook: 3-5 posts per week
- Threads: 3-5 posts per week
- YouTube: 1-2 videos per week if doing video

## The Rule That Makes It Stick
Posting reactively burns out. Batching 2-3 hours once a week and scheduling the rest is the only habit that survives month two.
