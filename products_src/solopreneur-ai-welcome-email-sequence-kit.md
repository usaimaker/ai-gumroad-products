# The Solopreneur's AI Welcome Email Sequence Kit

A free, copy-paste kit to build the 5-email welcome sequence that turns a new subscriber into a buyer — using AI, in under an hour.

## Why this matters
The welcome sequence is the highest-open-rate series you will ever send. Someone just gave you their email; they are paying attention for about 5 minutes. Waste it and they forget you. Nail it and you build trust that makes your first paid offer a no-brainer.

## The 5-email welcome sequence

| # | Goal | Angle | What to include |
|---|------|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Deliver + instant win | Here is what you asked for | Send the lead magnet link, one quick tip they can use today, sign off as a real person |
| 2 | Story + credibility | Why I do this | Your origin story in 3 sentences: the mistake you made, what you learned, the result you now get for clients |
| 3 | Problem agitation | The cost of doing nothing | Name the number one mistake in your niche, show the hidden cost, hint that you have a fix |
| 4 | Value drop | Steal this | One free tactic or template that works without you, soft bridge to your paid offer |
| 5 | The offer | Here is the next step | Clear paid offer, the outcome, a price anchor, one CTA, light urgency |

## Copy-paste AI prompts
Use these in ChatGPT or Claude. Replace the brackets.

**Prompt 1 - Email 1 (delivery):**
```
Write email 1 of a welcome sequence for my [niche] business. The subscriber just downloaded my free [lead magnet]. Goal: deliver it, give one quick win they can use today, and sound like a real human. Tone: warm, short. Under 150 words.
```

**Prompt 2 - Email 2 (story):**
```
Write email 2 of a welcome sequence. Share my founder story in 3 sentences for a [niche] audience: the mistake I made, what I learned, the result I now get for clients. Tone: honest, relatable. Under 180 words.
```

**Prompt 3 - Email 3 (problem):**
```
Write email 3 of a welcome sequence for [niche]. Name the single most expensive mistake your audience makes, show the hidden cost over 12 months, and hint you have a system that fixes it. No hard sell. Under 170 words.
```

**Prompt 4 - Email 4 (value):**
```
Write email 4 of a welcome sequence for [niche]. Teach one free tactic they can apply today without buying anything. Then bridge softly to my paid [offer] as the faster path. Under 200 words.
```

**Prompt 5 - Email 5 (offer):**
```
Write email 5 of a welcome sequence: the offer email. Present my [offer] at [price]. Show the outcome, one price anchor, a single clear CTA, and light urgency (enrollment closes or bonus ends). Under 200 words.
```

## The ask-for-the-sale rule
Never send more than two nurture-only emails before the offer. Most solopreneurs wait six months and lose the subscriber. Email 5 should ask clearly. If it feels pushy, you waited too long.
