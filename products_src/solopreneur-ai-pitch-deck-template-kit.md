# Solopreneur AI Pitch Deck Template Kit

A pitch deck is not a document. It is a 10-slide decision machine: every slide answers one question the listener already has. This kit gives you the slide order, the exact words to place on each slide, 12 copy-paste AI prompts, and a 60-minute build sprint. Use it for client pitches, partner deals, or a small raise.

## 1. Pick your deck type

| Scenario | Audience | Their real question | Emphasize slides |
|---|---|---|---|
| Client project pitch | Prospect or buyer | Can you solve my problem, and what will it cost? | 2, 4, 6, 7 |
| Partner / JV pitch | Founder with an audience | What do I get, and what must I do? | 2, 5, 7, 9 |
| Small investor raise | Angel or early backer | Why this, why you, and what is the upside? | 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 |

## 2. The 10-slide skeleton

| # | Slide | One job | What goes on it | Max words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Say what this is | Offering name, one-line outcome, your name, date | 12 |
| 2 | Problem | Make them nod | One specific pain, who has it, what it costs today | 30 |
| 3 | Stakes | Show why now | Cost of doing nothing: time, money, risk | 25 |
| 4 | Solution | Show the mechanism | What you deliver, how it works in 3 steps | 40 |
| 5 | How it works | Remove doubt | A 3-step flow or before/after snapshot | 30 |
| 6 | Proof | Earn trust | 3 proof points: a result, a number, a named process | 35 |
| 7 | Offer and price | Make it concrete | Scope, deliverables, price, exclusions | 45 |
| 8 | Traction | Show momentum | One signal: waitlist, past sales, trend, timing | 30 |
| 9 | The ask | Say what you want | One decision: sign, pay, introduce, or fund | 20 |
| 10 | Next step | Make starting easy | One action, one date, one contact | 15 |

Rule: one idea per slide. If a slide needs two headlines, split it.

## 3. One-line value proposition

Formula: I help [specific buyer] get [specific result] without [main pain], in [time frame].

| Fill-in example | Best for |
|---|---|
| I help independent coaches fill their calendar without daily cold DMs, in 30 days. | Client pitch |
| I help newsletter owners add a revenue stream without a product launch, in one quarter. | Partner pitch |
| I help local shops automate order follow-ups without hiring staff, in two weeks. | Investor pitch |

## 4. Twelve copy-paste AI prompts

| # | Prompt (paste into any AI chat) | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Act as a skeptical buyer. Read my one-line offer: [paste]. List the 5 objections you would raise before paying. | Slide 2 |
| 2 | Rewrite this problem statement so a busy owner recognizes it in 5 seconds: [paste]. Keep it under 30 words. | Slide 2 |
| 3 | Give me 3 versions of the cost of doing nothing for [buyer] facing [problem]. Use numbers where possible. | Slide 3 |
| 4 | Turn this feature list into a 3-step outcome flow a non-technical buyer can repeat from memory: [paste]. | Slide 4 |
| 5 | Write a before/after table comparing [current method] with [my method] across time, cost, and risk. | Slide 5 |
| 6 | Generate 10 proof-point templates I can fill in with my own results. No invented numbers; mark every blank clearly. | Slide 6 |
| 7 | Rewrite my offer as a 4-line scope: what is included, what is not, timeline, price. Plain language, no jargon. | Slide 7 |
| 8 | List 8 credible traction signals a solo founder can show before having big revenue. | Slide 8 |
| 9 | Write 3 versions of the ask: one for a client, one for a partner, one for an investor. One sentence each. | Slide 9 |
| 10 | Draft 5 low-friction next steps ranked by how easy they are for the listener to say yes to. | Slide 10 |
| 11 | Critique this 10-slide outline as a busy decision maker. Flag the 3 slides you would skip and why: [paste]. | Full deck |
| 12 | Compress each slide to 20 words or fewer without losing the point. Return a table: slide number, new text. | Full deck |

## 5. Proof cheat sheet

| If you have | Put on slide 6 | If you have nothing yet |
|---|---|---|
| Client results | Before/after metric with timeframe | Show a worked example you did for yourself |
| Testimonials | One quote with name and role | Show a screenshot of a real process or artifact |
| Sales history | Revenue, repeat rate, or count | Show a small waitlist or 5 conversations booked |
| Process quality | Named framework with 3 steps | Show a filled-in template, not a promise |

Never invent a number. A small real number beats a big fake one.

## 6. Q and A prep

| Likely question | Short answer frame |
|---|---|
| Why not do this myself? | You can. Here is the time and cost difference. |
| Why you? | One relevant proof point, then one sentence on method. |
| What if it fails? | Define the smallest reversible first step. |
| How much time does it take? | Give a number and what is included in it. |
| Can you do it cheaper? | Reduce scope first, never quality. |
| Who else has done this? | Name a comparable, then state your difference. |
| What happens after I say yes? | One dated next step. |
| Can I see an example? | Show a finished artifact, not a description. |

## 7. Five mistakes and fixes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| More than 40 words on a slide | Cut to one idea and one number |
| Features instead of outcomes | Lead with the buyer result, then the feature |
| No ask on slide 9 | Write the exact decision you want |
| Fake or vague proof | Use one real, small, specific number |
| Reading the deck aloud | Talk to the person, let slides carry the facts |

## 8. Sixty-minute build sprint

| Minutes | Action |
|---|---|
| 0-10 | Pick deck type and write the one-line value proposition |
| 10-25 | Fill the 10-slide skeleton with rough words only |
| 25-40 | Run prompts 1, 3, 6, 9, 12 and paste the best answers |
| 40-50 | Add proof from the cheat sheet, one real number minimum |
| 50-60 | Run the five-mistakes check, then rehearse the ask out loud |

## 9. Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Slide 1 states the outcome, not just a logo
- [ ] Slide 2 names one specific pain
- [ ] Slide 6 has at least one real number
- [ ] Slide 7 lists what is not included
- [ ] Slide 9 asks for one decision
- [ ] Slide 10 has a date
- [ ] No slide over 45 words
- [ ] Every blank is filled, no placeholders left
