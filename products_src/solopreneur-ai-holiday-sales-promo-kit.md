## The Margin-Safe Discount Ladder
Never discount blind. Pick the deepest cut your margin survives, then stack one reason to buy now.
| Offer type | Use when | Margin guard |
|------------|----------|-------------|
| Flat % off (10-20%) | List-wide, low effort | Cap at break-even minus fees |
| Bundle (2-for-1 / 3-get-1) | Move slow SKUs | Bundle at full price, discount the "free" unit only |
| Spend threshold (free ship over $X) | Lift AOV | Set X = current AOV x 1.3 |
| Early access (past buyers) | Reward loyalty | 24h head start, smaller % |
| Last-day flash | Recover abandoned carts | One window, no extension |

**Quick math:** sale price minus (cost + payment fee% + shipping) must stay above 0. If margin goes negative, switch to a bundle or free-gift instead of a deeper cut.
