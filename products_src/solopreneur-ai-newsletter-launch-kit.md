# The Solopreneur's AI Newsletter Launch Kit

Launch a profitable newsletter from zero - even with no audience yet. This kit gives you a niche finder, a 5-email welcome sequence, a 90-day content calendar, a reusable issue template, a growth playbook with copy-paste AI prompts, and a monetization map. No course, no fluff - open it and start sending this week.

## 1. Niche Finder (pick a winner in 10 minutes)

Pressure-test your idea before you write one issue. Score each row 1-5; if total is under 18, pivot.

| Question | What a winning answer looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Who is it for? | One specific role, not "everyone" (e.g. freelance designers) |
| What outcome? | A measurable result ("land 2 clients a month") |
| Can you prove it? | You have done it or have a client result |
| Are they searching? | 1k+ monthly searches or active communities |
| Will they pay? | They already buy tools or courses in this space |
| Can you write weekly? | You already have 20+ ideas |

**AI prompt - validate your niche:**
> Act as a newsletter growth strategist. My topic is [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. List 5 signs this niche is too broad or too small, name 3 competing newsletters, and give the one angle that would make mine indispensable. Be blunt.

## 2. The 5-Email Welcome Sequence

Send automatically on signup. Goal: train them to open, then make a soft pitch.

| # | Goal | Include | Soft CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Deliver + set expectation | The download link, send frequency | Reply with your biggest challenge |
| 2 | Prove credibility | One specific client result | Read my most-read issue |
| 3 | Teach a quick win | A tactic they can use today | Try it, reply with results |
| 4 | Share your story | The moment you found your insight | See your offer / book a call |
| 5 | Make the offer | Product or service, price, guarantee | Buy or reply "info" |

**AI prompts - write each email fast:**
> Write email 1 of a welcome sequence for my [NICHE] newsletter. Deliver the free [LEAD MAGNET]. Warm, 90 words, one question at the end.
> Write email 4: my founder story for a [NICHE] audience. Show the moment I realized [INSIGHT]. 120 words, confident not salesy.

## 3. 90-Day Content Calendar

Send 1-2 times per week. Rotate themes so you never run dry.

| Week | Theme | Content type | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-2 | Foundations | How-to + checklist | Teach the basics |
| 3-4 | Quick wins | Tactical teardown | Build trust |
| 5-6 | Case study | Real result breakdown | Show proof |
| 7-8 | Tools | Stack or template | Save time |
| 9-10 | Q&A | Reader questions | Boost engagement |
| 11-12 | Offer | Soft pitch | Drive revenue |
| 13+ | Repeat deeper | Mix of the above | Compound reach |

## 4. The Reusable Issue Template

Paste this skeleton for every send - stays consistent in 30 minutes:

1. Hook (1 line): the payoff or the mistake
2. Context (2 lines): why it matters now
3. The meat (300-500 words): one idea, one example
4. Action step: the one thing to do today
5. Sign-off + CTA: link to offer or reply prompt

**AI prompt - draft an issue:**
> Using this skeleton [HOOK / CONTEXT / MEAT / ACTION / CTA], write this week's [NICHE] newsletter issue about [TOPIC]. 400 words, plain English, one clear action step.
