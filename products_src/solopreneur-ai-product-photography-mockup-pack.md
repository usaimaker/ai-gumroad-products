## The 4-step studio workflow
1. Capture a flat, evenly lit phone photo on a plain background (daylight near a window works best).
2. Isolate the product with Photoroom or Remove.bg to strip the background in about 5 seconds.
3. Stage the product in a lifestyle scene with Pebblely or Flair AI (see the prompt table below).
4. Mock it up by placing your design on a real product with Mockey or Recraft (packaging, apparel, books, mugs).

## Listing-ready checklist
- Main image: pure white, product 85 percent plus of frame (Amazon and Etsy compliant)
- At least 3 lifestyle scenes showing the product in use
- Size chart or scale reference where relevant
- Consistent brand color across all images
- Mobile-zoom clear, no blur when pinched
- One gift or premium hero shot
- Exported at 2000 pixels plus on the long edge, RGB, under 2 MB

## 7-day launch sprint
- Day 1: Capture raw phone photos of every SKU on a plain background.
- Day 2: Background-remove all with Photoroom or Remove.bg.
- Day 3: Generate 3 lifestyle scenes per product with Pebblely.
- Day 4: Build mockups with Mockey or Recraft for any design-based SKUs.
- Day 5: Run prompts 1 to 4 to write titles, descriptions, and captions.
- Day 6: Assemble listings using the checklist above.
- Day 7: Publish to Etsy, Gumroad, and Amazon, then schedule social posts.
