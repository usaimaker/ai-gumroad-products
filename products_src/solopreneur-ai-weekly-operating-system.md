# The Solopreneur's AI Weekly Operating System

A one-page weekly planning system for solo founders. Copy the prompts, fill the tables, and run the same 60-minute loop every week. No paid app required.

## The 5 Blocks

| Block | When | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capture | Fri | 15 min | One messy list of every open task, idea, loose end |
| Prioritize | Fri | 20 min | 3 outcomes (not 10 tasks) via the filter below |
| Time-block | Fri | 15 min | 10-12 protected calendar slots |
| Execute | Mon-Fri | Daily | Work the blocks, not the inbox |
| Review | Fri | 10 min | Score the week, carry over at most 1 item |

## Priority Filter

Score each task 0-2 on Impact, 0-2 on Urgency, 0-2 on Energy fit. Keep only tasks scoring 5+, capped at 3 outcomes. Everything else goes to Someday.

| Task | Impact | Urgency | Energy | Total | Keep? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Send proposal to Acme | 2 | 2 | 2 | 6 | Yes |
| Redesign logo | 1 | 0 | 1 | 2 | Someday |

## The 60-Minute Weekly Loop

1. (15 min) Capture: brain-dump everything into one list. No filtering.
2. (20 min) Prioritize: run the filter. Pick 3 outcomes, not 10 tasks.
3. (15 min) Time-block: put each outcome into 90-minute slots. Protect them like client calls.
4. (10 min) Pre-commit: write Monday's first block on a sticky note. Start there.

## Friday Review Checklist

- [ ] Finished 3 outcomes? If not, was it estimate, interruption, or wrong outcome?
- [ ] Which block got skipped most? Fix the cause, not the symptom.
- [ ] What did I do that only I can do? Protect it next week.
- [ ] What can I automate, delegate, or delete?
- [ ] Carry over at most 1 leftover task.

## Three Rules

- Three outcomes per week. A fourth means nothing is prioritized.
- Deep-work blocks are immovable. Admin flexes around them.
- If a task sits on Someday three weeks running, delete it.
