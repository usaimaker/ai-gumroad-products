# The Solopreneur's AI Client Proposal Kit

A good proposal makes the right client say yes without a long sales call. This kit gives you a repeatable 7-part structure, 12 copy-paste AI prompts, a scope-and-price table, objection swipe, and a 24-hour follow-up system. Customize the brackets, send, close.

## Why proposals lose (fix first)
- Too much "about us", too little "about their outcome" -> lead with their result.
- Vague scope -> ambiguous scope causes 70% of project disputes. Define deliverables and exclusions.
- No price anchor -> show 3 tiers; clients pick the middle by default.
- No next step -> every proposal ends with one clear action and a date.

## The 7-part proposal skeleton
| # | Section | Job | Target length |
|---|---------|-----|---------------|
| 1 | Header + one-line outcome | Say what changes for them | 1 line |
| 2 | Their problem (in their words) | Prove you listened | 3-5 bullets |
| 3 | Proposed solution | Map problem -> your method | 1 short paragraph |
| 4 | Deliverables + exclusions | Kill scope creep | Table (below) |
| 5 | Timeline / milestones | Set pace and trust | 3-5 rows |
| 6 | Investment (3 tiers) | Anchor and choose | Table (below) |
| 7 | Next step + CTA | Get the yes | 2 lines |

## 12 copy-paste AI prompts
Replace brackets only. Paste into any AI assistant.
| # | Use it for | Prompt |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Outcome line | "Write one 12-word sentence describing the result a [client type] gets from [your service]. Lead with a measurable outcome. No jargon." |
| 2 | Problem section | "List 5 problems a [client type] has with [problem area] before hiring help. Write each as one punchy bullet in their own words." |
| 3 | Solution blur | "In 60 words, explain how [your method] solves [problem]. Use 'you', present tense, plain English. No buzzwords." |
| 4 | Deliverables | "Turn this service [describe it] into a deliverables table: item, format, quantity, due milestone. Add a separate 'not included' column with 4 likely exclusions." |
| 5 | Timeline | "Create a 4-week milestone plan for [project]. Columns: week, milestone, client input needed, deliverable." |
| 6 | Tier names | "Name 3 service tiers (good/better/best) for [client type]. Names must describe outcomes, not sizes. One word each." |
| 7 | Tier copy | "For each tier [list features], write a 1-line promise and one 'best for' clause. Keep under 15 words each." |
| 8 | Value anchor | "Write 3 short lines framing the cost of doing nothing about [problem] in money and time terms. Specific, not fear-based." |
| 9 | Objection swipe | "Write 4 concise responses to: too expensive, no time, need to think, we tried this before. Empathetic, 2 sentences, no pressure." |
| 10 | Case-study line | "Rewrite this client result [paste result] into one proof line: starting situation -> action -> measurable outcome. Under 20 words." |
| 11 | Proposal polish | "Edit this proposal for clarity and confidence. Cut 20% of words, remove passive voice, keep all numbers and brackets." |
| 12 | Follow-up email | "Write a 90-word follow-up for a proposal sent 24h ago. Reference their goal, restate one deliverable, ask one yes/no question." |

## 3 pricing structures that close
| Structure | How it works | Best for |
|-----------|--------------|----------|
| Fixed scope | One price, defined deliverables | Clear, bounded projects |
| Tiered (3 options) | Good / better / best packages | Most service businesses |
| Retainer | Monthly fee for ongoing work | Recurring needs |

## Objection-handling swipe
| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "Too expensive" | "I hear you. Compared to what, specifically? Let's map the cost of it staying unsolved." |
| "No time" | "That is exactly why this is scoped to [#] hours of your involvement total." |
| "Need to think" | "Of course. What one question can I answer today to make that easier?" |
| "Tried this before" | "What broke last time? I built [specific guard] into this scope so it doesn't repeat." |

## 24-hour follow-up sequence
1. +0 min: send proposal + one-line "reply YES to start".
2. +24 h: send prompt #12 follow-up email.
3. +72 h: add one proof line (prompt #10) and a 48-hour decision window.
4. +7 days: close the file politely; offer to revisit next quarter.

## 10-minute pre-send checklist
- [ ] Outcome line is about them, not you
- [ ] Every bracket replaced
- [ ] Deliverables and exclusions both listed
- [ ] Three tiers with one recommended
- [ ] Timeline has real dates
- [ ] One proof line included
- [ ] One clear CTA with a deadline
- [ ] Read aloud once; cut any jargon
