## 10 copy-paste AI prompts
Use these in ChatGPT or Claude. Replace the [brackets] with your niche.

1. Niche validator - "List 10 low-competition affiliate niches in [pet supplies]. For each, give monthly search intent, 3 affiliate programs, and a realistic first-year commission range."
2. Review writer - "Write a 600-word honest review of [Product X] for [busy dog owners]. Cover what it does, who it is for, one real drawback, and a clear recommendation. Tone: helpful, not salesy."
3. Comparison post - "Create a Product X vs Product Y comparison table for [AI writing tools] with 6 decision factors and a best-for verdict for each reader type."
4. Program finder - "Find 8 high-commission affiliate programs in [home fitness] with signup links, payout thresholds, and whether they accept beginner applicants."
5. SEO brief - "Build an SEO content brief for the keyword [best ergonomic chairs 2026]: search intent, 8 secondary keywords, FAQ schema questions, and a 1200-word outline."
6. Email promo - "Write a 3-email affiliate promo sequence for [budgeting app]. Email 1 educates, email 2 shows results, email 3 limited offer. No hype, clear disclosure."
7. Pinterest pin - "Write 10 Pinterest pin titles plus descriptions promoting my [meal-planning template] lead magnet. Add 3 hashtags each and a curiosity hook."
8. Short video script - "Write a 45-second TikTok or Reels script reviewing [wireless earbuds] with a hook, 3 quick benefits, one con, and a soft CTA to my link."
9. Subject lines - "Generate 15 email subject lines for a newsletter promoting [VPN deal]. Mix curiosity, urgency, and plain-benefit styles. Under 50 characters each."
10. Disclosure and compliance - "Draft an FTC-compliant affiliate disclosure paragraph for my [blog or newsletter] that reads naturally and meets clear and conspicuous rules."

## The FTC disclosure swipe (copy-paste)
"This post contains affiliate links. If you buy through them I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. I only recommend tools I would use myself. Full disclaimer: [link]."

## 3 rookie mistakes to avoid
- Promoting everything - 20 programs equals zero trust. Start with 2-3.
- Skipping disclosure - the FTC requires clear, conspicuous disclosure. Always.
- Chasing commissions over fit - promote what your audience actually needs.
