# The Solopreneur's AI Joint Venture Outreach Kit

Borrow someone else's audience instead of renting it. Joint ventures (JVs) — bundles, summits, affiliate swaps, and co-launches — are the fastest zero-ad-spend growth lever for a one-person business in 2026. This kit gives you the system, the scripts, and the AI prompts to land 10+ partners in 14 days.

## Why JVs Outperform Paid Ads
- **Warm reach:** you show up in front of a list that already trusts someone.
- **$0 media spend:** no CPMs, no pixel, no ad fatigue.
- **Instant authority:** a peer endorsement beats a cold ad.
- **Compounding:** every partner becomes a long-term affiliate.

## 1. Pick Your JV Type

| JV Type | Best When | Ideal Partner | Your Effort | Reach Multiple |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product Bundle | You have a paid product | Non-competing product owners | Medium | 3-10x |
| Summit / Challenge | You want emails fast | Creators with 2k-50k list | High | 5-20x |
| Affiliate Swap | You have an ongoing offer | Recurring-commission peers | Low | 2-5x |
| Guest Expert | You want authority | Podcasts, newsletters, communities | Low | 1-3x |
| Co-Launch | You have a launch window | Adjacent-launch creators | High | 5-15x |

## 2. The 5-Step JV Outreach System
1. **Map your asset** - list what you bring (list size, product, audience niche).
2. **Build a 50-partner hit list** - score each on fit + reach.
3. **Send a 1-tap pitch** - short, specific, with a clear yes path.
4. **Negotiate terms** - commission, timeline, who sends what.
5. **Launch & track** - shared tracker, unique links, post-mortem.

Use these inside your LLM of choice. Replace the [brackets].

| 11 | Tracker | "Build a CSV schema to track 50 JV partners: name, status, reach, link, commission, sent date, result." |
