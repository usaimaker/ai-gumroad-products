# Solopreneur AI Case Study Builder Kit

Turn one client result into a proof asset that sells for you around the clock. Case studies are the highest-trust lead magnet: they prove possibility, not theory. This kit gives you the exact structure, interview questions, and copy-paste AI prompts to publish a sharp one-page case study in a single afternoon.

## 1. Pick the right story (2-minute selector)

| Situation | Case study type | What to prove |
|---|---|---|
| First client ever | From zero to first result | You can deliver at all |
| Client scaled a metric | Number-up story | Measurable ROI |
| Client escaped a pain | Before/after story | Relief and speed |
| Client switched provider | Why they switched | Your differentiation |
| Several small wins | Pattern story (3 clients) | Consistency |

Rule: one case study = one transformation = one number. Never bundle three outcomes.

## 2. The 7-part skeleton

| # | Section | Words | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Headline (result + timeframe) | 8-15 | Stop the scroll |
| 2 | Snapshot box | 40 | Instant context |
| 3 | The problem in their words | 80-120 | Create identification |
| 4 | The turning point | 40 | Build tension |
| 5 | What you did (3 steps max) | 120-180 | Show your method |
| 6 | The result (numbers + quote) | 60-100 | Prove it |
| 7 | CTA (one action) | 20 | Convert reader |

## 4. The 12-question client interview

1. What was happening before we started that made you look for help?
2. What had you already tried, and why did it not work?
3. What was the cost of leaving the problem unsolved?
4. What made you say yes to working together?
5. What were you skeptical about at the start?
6. What did we do in week one?
7. Which part of the process surprised you?
8. What changed first?
9. Which exact numbers moved, and by how much?
10. How long did the change take?
11. What would you tell someone considering the same thing?
12. May I quote you on that?

## 5. Copy-paste AI prompts (10)

1. Turn these raw interview notes into a 7-part case study using the skeleton above. Under 500 words, plain language, no hype adjectives.
2. Rewrite this headline 12 ways using: [specific result] in [timeframe] without [common objection].
3. Pull every number from these notes into a before/after table. Flag any number missing a baseline.
4. Tighten this client quote into 3 versions that keep the voice but cut filler.
5. Write the problem section using only phrases from the transcript.
6. Explain my 3-step method to a skeptical beginner in 120 words, no jargon.
7. List 10 objections a reader might have and one line answering each.
8. Write 5 final CTAs: soft, direct, curiosity, scarcity, and question-led.
9. Convert this case study into a 5-post LinkedIn thread, one idea per post.
10. Score this case study 1-10 on proof, specificity, and readability, then rewrite the weakest section.

## 6. Proof rules (do not skip)

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Use real numbers, or label it qualitative | Invented stats destroy trust |
| Get written quote approval | Avoids awkward corrections later |
| Name the niche, not just the logo | Relatability beats prestige |
| Show the method, not just the win | Builds credibility for your offer |
| One CTA only | More links mean fewer clicks |
