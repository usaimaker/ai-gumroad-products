# The Solopreneur's AI Content Upgrade Kit

A content upgrade is a free bonus you attach to a single blog post, video, or newsletter issue - so relevant that readers hand over their email on the spot. Generic site-wide opt-ins convert at 1-2%. Content upgrades convert at 5-10% (and up to 10x higher on the right post). This kit gives you the exact system to spin one out in under 30 minutes using AI.

## Why this beats a generic lead magnet
- Relevance at the point of intent: the reader just consumed your content, so the bonus for THAT content lands hard.
- Zero new audience needed: you mine the traffic you already have.
- AI does the heavy lifting: research, draft, design prompt, and write the delivery email in minutes.

## 6-step build process (under 30 min)
1. Identify the one action the reader wants after this post.
2. Pick the upgrade type from the table above.
3. Use the AI prompts below to draft the asset.
4. Export to PDF (Canva free) or a single Google Doc.
5. Write the 1-email delivery sequence (prompt below).
6. Add the inline opt-in + end-of-post CTA, then publish.

## 10 copy-paste AI prompts

| # | Use it for | Prompt |
|---|------------|--------|
| 1 | Topic fit | "Given this blog post about [TOPIC], what is the single most useful checklist a reader would want right after reading it? List 10 options." |
| 2 | Draft checklist | "Write a 10-item actionable checklist titled '[TITLE]' for a solopreneur. Each item = one line + why it matters." |
| 3 | Draft template | "Create a fill-in-the-blank [TYPE] template (e.g. proposal, brief, invoice) a solo freelancer can use today." |
| 4 | Draft swipe file | "Give me 15 proven [SUBJECT LINE / HOOK / OUTREACH] examples for [NICHE]. One per line, no fluff." |
| 5 | Build calculator | "Design a simple spreadsheet formula set to calculate [METRIC] for a solopreneur. List each cell and formula." |
| 6 | Delivery email | "Write a 120-word email that delivers [UPGRADE] and asks the reader one qualifying question." |
| 7 | CTA copy | "Write 3 short in-post CTA sentences for offering [UPGRADE] as a content upgrade. Friendly, no hype." |
| 8 | Landing line | "Write a one-line opt-in headline: [OUTCOME] for [AUDIENCE] - get [UPGRADE] free." |
| 9 | Repurpose | "Turn this checklist into 5 social posts that each tease one item and link to the upgrade." |
| 10 | A/B test | "Give me 2 subject lines and 2 button texts to A/B test the upgrade opt-in. Keep under 40 chars." |
