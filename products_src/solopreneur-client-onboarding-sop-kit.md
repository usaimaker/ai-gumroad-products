## The 6-Step Onboarding Flow

| # | Step | What happens | Tool | AI prompt |
|---|------|--------------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Confirm and welcome | Send welcome email plus contract | Gmail or HelloSign | Prompt 1 |
| 2 | Intake | Client fills questionnaire | Google Form | Prompt 2 |
| 3 | Kickoff | 20-minute call, scope locked | Zoom | Prompt 3 |
| 4 | Setup | Workspace and shared board | Notion or Trello | Prompt 4 |
| 5 | First deliverable | Ship within 72 hours | - | Prompt 5 |
| 6 | Review and upsell | Check-in plus offer add-on | Email | Prompt 1 |

## Copy-Paste Client Emails

| Stage | Subject line | What it does |
|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Welcome | You are in - here is what happens next | Sets expectations and builds trust |
| Intake | 2 minutes to tailor this to you | Drives questionnaire completion |
| Kickoff | Your kickoff call - pick a time | Books the call and reduces no-shows |
| Handoff | Your first deliverable is ready | Proves value fast |
| Upsell | Want to go further? | Opens the next sale |

## Client Intake Questionnaire (fill-in)

1. What is the number one result you need in the next 30 days?
2. What has already failed to fix this?
3. What does done look like to you?
4. Any hard deadlines or non-negotiables?
5. Who else is involved in the decision?
6. What is your biggest worry about working together?

## Anti-Chaos Launch Checklist

- [ ] Welcome email scheduled before payment clears
- [ ] Contract auto-sent on purchase
- [ ] Questionnaire live and linked
- [ ] Kickoff calendar invite templated
- [ ] Shared workspace created per client
- [ ] First deliverable deadline set at 72 hours or less
- [ ] Upsell email queued for day 14

## 7-Day Rollout Plan

- Day 1: Paste emails into your ESP, swap placeholders
- Day 2: Build the Google Form from the questionnaire
- Day 3: Set up the Notion or Trello board
- Day 4: Run the 5 AI prompts for your niche
- Day 5: Wire automations (purchase to welcome to form)
- Day 6: Dry-run with a friend
- Day 7: Go live
